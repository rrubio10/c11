PARSER RULES
1. A set is enclosed by [SET] ... [/SET].
2. The complete exercise/passage is inside text_begin ... text_end.
3. Each answer is enclosed by [ITEM] ... [/ITEM].
4. Compare answers case-insensitively after trimming spaces.
5. For accepted_answers, split on " | ".
6. For P1/P5/P6/P7/P8, correct_answer is a letter.
7. For P2/P3/P4, compare against accepted_answers.
8. Do not reveal correct_answer until the learner submits an answer.

SCORING
- Official-style Part 4 and Reading Parts 5–7 use max_points=2.
- Other official-style items use max_points=1.
- The standalone and Mega transformation practice sets use max_points=1 per item.
